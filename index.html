About me

I study how to represent medical images more efficiently while preserving every voxel exactly.

I am a PhD student at Wuhan University of Technology, China. My doctoral research focuses on lossless 3D medical image compression and the geometric and statistical structure of volumetric data.

My broader interests include deep learning, computer vision, semantic segmentation, and visual representation learning. Before my doctoral studies, I completed a master's degree in Computer Science and worked on image analysis and learning-based recognition systems.
