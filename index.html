About me

I study how to represent medical images more efficiently while preserving every voxel exactly.

I am a PhD student at Wuhan University of Technology, China. My doctoral research focuses on lossless 3D medical image compression, with an emphasis on learned probability models and the spatial structure of volumetric data.

My broader interests include deep learning, computer vision, semantic segmentation, and visual representation learning. Before my doctoral studies, I completed a master's degree in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Technology and a B.Sc. with Honours in Computer Science at the University of The Gambia.
