About me

I am a doctoral student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Junwei Zhou. My research focuses on lossless compression of 3D medical images. I study the geometric structure of image volumes and the statistical relationships between neighboring voxels to reduce storage requirements while preserving every original voxel value.

I earned my master’s degree in Computer Science and Technology at Wuhan University of Technology. I hold a B.Sc. with Honours in Computer Science from the University of The Gambia. Before my doctoral studies, I worked as a data analyst, a radio-frequency engineer, and an information technology teacher.
